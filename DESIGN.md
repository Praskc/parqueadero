# Design Direction: Campus Puerta Roja (Universidad de Sucre)

## Product Context
Public kiosk display and guardhouse operator console for real-time vehicle occupancy monitoring (30 bays across 3 institutional groups: Estudiantes `E01 a E10`, Docentes `E11 a E20`, Administrativos `E21 a E30`) and barrier gate control via MQTT/WebSocket.

## Liveliness Dials (Antislop Part 3)
- **ENERGY: 1 (Calm)**: Public-service transit and campus wayfinding board; high legibility at a glance without promotional clutter.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Balanced)**: Structured 3-tier operational hierarchy: top status header, dominant architectural site plan (`1200x580` SVG), and 4-column breakdown strip.
- **MOTION: 2 (Functional transitions & owner-directed savanna breeze)**: Motion represents physical events (vehicles transiting lanes, barrier arm rotating `0°` to `-85°`, progress bar width updates) plus an owner-requested subtle wind sway (`<= 2.6°` transform rotation, paused automatically under `prefers-reduced-motion` and `Reducir movimiento`) so dense savanna grass filaments do not read as frozen (`R-19`, `R-37`). Static LED pulses remain prohibited.

## Color System (R-29: 3 Core Colors + Architectural Neutrals, ISA-101 HMI)
- **Primary (`#15803d`, Verde Institucional Unisucre):** Indicates available bays (`LIBRE`), nominal occupancy (`< 65%`), active connection, and open barrier passage.
- **Secondary (`#eab308` / `#facc15` bars & indicators, `#8a6d00` WCAG AA text on white, Amarillo Operativo):** Marks elevated occupancy (`65%` to `84%`), `EN CAMINO` assigned bays, barrier closing warning, stale sensor warnings, and waiting-queue states in true traffic yellow (`R-37` owner direction, replacing amber).
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#dc2626`, Rojo Puerta Roja):** Marks occupied bays (`OCUPADO`), critical capacity (`>= 85%` occupancy or `<= 4` free bays out of `30`), and locked barrier states.
- **Neutrals, Institutional Footer & Tropical Savanna Landscaping (`#0f172a`, `#1e293b`, `#334155`, `#475569`, `#f1f5f9`, `#ffffff`, institutional footer `#184320` / `#f3e69f`, sunlit savanna grass `#1b400f`..`#d9f99d`, weathered limestone rocks `#f5f5f4` / `#a8a29e` / `#57534e`, and *árbol de pimiento* drupes `#e11d48` / `#fda4af`):** Institutional light canvas (`#f1f5f9` / `#ffffff`) by default with full dark-mode parity (`#0b1120` / `#0f172a`, `R-34`) toggled from inside the floating `Accesibilidad` dialog (`R-21`, `R-37`), paired with dense multi-filament grass, directional NW-to-SE cast shadows, varied limestone outcrops, and feathery *árbol de pimiento* (*Schinus*) canopies outside concrete curbs.

## Typography (R-06)
- **Display & Signage (`Chakra Petch`, `Barlow`):** Self-hosted via `@fontsource`; geometric transit-board character suited to wayfinding headers and large numeric readouts.
- **Body & Controls (`Barlow`):** Self-hosted humanist-grotesque transit sans sized at `14px` to `18px` for secondary labels so operators can read modalities, counts, and status readouts at gatehouse monitor distance.
- **Telemetry & Codes (`JetBrains Mono` with `tabular-nums`):** Strictly for bay identifiers (`E01` to `E30`), live clock, date with year, occupancy percentages, and MQTT topics so digits never shift layout width.
- **Accessibility Reading Overrides (`Atkinson Hyperlegible`, `OpenDyslexic`):** Self-hosted via `@fontsource` and switchable from the `Accesibilidad` dialog (`Fuente legible` and `Letra para dislexia` with `font-size-adjust: 0.45` across HTML and SVG `<text>`) so drivers and operators with low vision or dyslexia can read signage without breaking SVG bay geometry.

## Architectural Decisions (R-31, R-07, R-37)
- **Proportional 1920x1080 single-screen fit with full-width institutional header & below-the-fold 4-column footer (`R-03`, `R-05`, `R-31`, `R-37`):** The top `min-h-dvh` frame begins with an edge-to-edge Universidad de Sucre `<header>` bar (`#184320` light / `#133519` dark) followed by the centered `max-w-[1340px]` operational workspace (`<ParkingMap>` and 4-column `<section>` breakdown), keeping the entire dashboard visible without scroll on `1920x1080`, while the official institutional footer (`#184320`, structured in 4 distinct columns: Unisucre emblem/NIT, `Contáctenos`, `Visitas` + `Síguenos`, and `¿Encontró algún bug?` with GitHub + institutional `mailto:` envelope links for `Esteban Eduardo Cotera Prasca` and `Pedro José Mercado Salazar`, plus envelope `mailto:` links for `Angie Torrecilla Pineda` and `Diego Andrés Acosta Durán` using `Nombre.PrimerApellido@unisucrevirtual.edu.co` per owner instruction `R-37`) sits strictly below `100dvh` so it appears only when scrolling down.
- **Capsule header with separated clock, 2-pane operator console & twin bottom-right floating FABs (`R-11`, `R-12`, `R-19`, `R-20`, `R-21`, `R-31`, `R-34`, `R-37`):** Per owner visual reference (`R-37`), `<header>` places the Unisucre shield and wordmark on the left, a single centered high-contrast `lg:rounded-full` availability island (`#ffffff` light / `#0f172a` dark) dedicated exclusively to `Cupos libres`, occupied/en-route counts, and sensor freshness, and on the right side of the green bar a separated tabular clock/date (`#ffffff` / `#f3e69f`) beside the circular `46x46 px` `rounded-full` `Panel de control` button opening the 2-pane operator console. At bottom-right, the circular `48x48 px` `Accesibilidad` FAB (`fixed bottom-[16px] right-[16px] sm:bottom-[20px] sm:right-[20px] z-40`) with an active-adjustments badge is joined by a matching circular `48x48 px` `Volver arriba` FAB (`fixed bottom-[16px] right-[76px] sm:bottom-[20px] sm:right-[80px] z-30`) that launches via compositor transform/opacity transition when scrolled to the bottom of the document and smoothly returns to `top: 0` (instant under `Reducir movimiento`).
- **Comprehensive Accessibility Suite (`R-21`, `R-25`, `R-32`, `R-34`, `R-37`):** The `Accesibilidad` panel groups `Tamaño de texto` (`100%` to `200%`), `Lectura y enlaces` (`Letra para dislexia`, `Fuente legible`, `Espaciado amplio` per WCAG 1.4.12, and `Enlaces subrayados` with `2.5px` high-visibility underlines), and `Color, contraste y orientación` (`Modo oscuro`, `Alto contraste`, `Escala de grises` scoped via `.superficie-accesible` so `position: fixed` controls never lose viewport anchoring, `Puntero y foco destacados`, and `Reducir movimiento`).
- **Planimetric drafting-grid ground (`R-07` purpose + `R-37` owner override):** The outer canvas uses a subtle `28x28 px` orthogonal drafting grid (`.bg-campus-surface` in light and dark modes) to ground the `1200x580` civil CAD site plan as an engineering plan sheet, explicitly kept per owner instruction (`R-37`).
- **Pure top-down architectural guardhouse, thermoplastic pavement arrows & classic *árbol de pimiento* savanna landscaping (`R-19`, `R-20`, `R-31`, `R-37`):** Vertical elevation traffic signs are omitted (`R-37`) to preserve strict cenital perspective; circulation is guided by elongated thermoplastic roadway stencils (`#flecha-vial-recta`, `#flecha-vial-curva-retorno` centered at `x=1170` in the eastern return lane clear of central-island vegetation, and combined straight+turn stencils on Carrera 28) on clean unsplit one-way aisles. The gatehouse (`PORTERÍA`) is drawn in true top-down architectural plan view, while surrounding parklands feature double-layered dense multi-filament grass turf, breeze-swaying filament tussocks, 6 distinct karstic/stratified limestone rockery archetypes (*caliza sabanera*), and classic volumetric rounded-umbellate *árbol de pimiento* (*Schinus molle*) canopies (`#arbol-pimiento-a/b/c/joven`) with weeping pinnate fringes, sunlit foliage domes, rosy peppercorn clusters, and NW-to-SE cast shadows rendered above the ground understory.
- **Visual row salience without auto-routing (`R-20`, `R-25`, `R-31`, `R-37`):** Because the diagram is a schematic layout rather than a 1:1 physical plan of the university (`R-37` owner direction), turn-by-turn lane routing is omitted; instead, whenever at least one zone has available spaces and another zone reaches `0 libres`, full zones recede into low-salience slate (`#151e2d` bay fill, `#334155` subtle strokes, dimmed parked silhouettes at `0.26` opacity, and recessed `#0f172a` plinth) while open rows stand out with `#092918` plinths and `#22c55e` row perimeter frames (`6.42:1` against `#1e293b` asphalt).
- **Full-bay state legibility, intermediate `EN CAMINO` yellow state & top-layer bay badges:** Active open-zone bays tint their entire surface (`LIBRE` `#113826`, `EN CAMINO` `#383206` + `#facc15` dashed border when assigned to an approaching vehicle, `OCUPADO` `#351218` with metallic CAD vehicle silhouette, `SIN DATO` diagonal slate pattern), display high-contrast uppercase status text, and place a `#0f172a` badge with `#ffffff` `JetBrains Mono` text (`17.85:1`) on the topmost layer so the map is self-explanatory without a redundant legend strip (`R-37`).
- **Clean inline zone headers without vehicle-type or count badges (`R-37`):** All three institutional zones use an identical `118x96` architectural signage plinth at the left start of their 10-bay row displaying strictly the group name (`Estudiantes`, `Docentes`, `Administrativos`) and bay range (`E01 a E10`, `E11 a E20`, `E21 a E30`) without `Motos`, `Mixto`, or `LIBRES` badges (`R-37`), while the bottom breakdown names each sector (`Ocupación parqueadero Estudiantes`, `Ocupación parqueadero Docentes`, `Ocupación parqueadero Administrativos`) with `Bahías E01 a E10`..`E21 a E30` and pairs with `Ocupar fila` / `Liberar fila` controls in `Panel de control`.
- **Hairline grid over nested cards:** Breakdown metrics use a single bordered container divided by `1px` hairlines instead of nested colored sub-cards.
